# W12 economy and wave-pacing model — summary

Map 1 (first slice), 20 waves, seed 20261006, 10 ticks per second. Generated by `npm run model`; do not edit by hand.

Resource triples are mass / energy / rare. "Slowed" counts seconds in which at least one build or research item progressed below full speed because a resource ran short, attributed to the scarcest resource that tick. "Worst ratio" is the lowest share of a tick's requested draw the pool could cover.

## Strategies

- **balanced**: Half the deposits first, a small north defence, lab and salvage chain by minute 5, east fortified before it opens, every defence type.
- **greedy**: All deposits, ten generators, three rare points and six engineers first; one gun turret until minute 8, then the balanced defence plan.
- **turtle**: Walls and gun turrets only, upgraded through wall and gun tiers; no salvage chain, heavy cannon, area cannon, beam or shield.
- **no-salvage**: The balanced plan with engineers never reclaiming, so the chain gets no input and heavy cannons get no parts.
