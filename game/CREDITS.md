# Credits — Bulwark game textures (W24 run 2a)

The texture sets in `public/assets/` are copied from `look/public/assets/` (W17, downloaded there by `look/scripts/fetch-assets.mjs` from the public APIs; no account was used). All nine sets are used by the game: the terrain material (cliff_side, sandy_gravel_02, rock_face), the W17 building and alien materials (Metal016, metal_plate_02, Metal047B, concrete_panels, rock_face, cliff_side), the W20/W23 turret, wall, extractor and unit materials (beige_wall_001) and the image-based light (rogland_sunset). Every asset is CC0 1.0 (public domain dedication); attribution is not required and is given anyway. Everything else in the scene (geometry, panel seams, sky, icons) is generated by code.

Total texture and HDRI bytes on disk: 36746484 (35.04 MiB).
